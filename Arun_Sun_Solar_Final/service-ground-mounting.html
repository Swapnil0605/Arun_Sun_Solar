Ground mounting installation & service

Our ground mounting scope brings together the site, structural, cable, equipment and testing activities required across the solar field.

01

Trench Marking

Marking the required trench routes before excavation and cable-related work across the solar project.

02

Trench Excavation & Cleaning

Excavation and preparation of trenches, including trench cleaning with brick and sand laying.

03

Cable Laying Work

Laying project cables through prepared routes as part of the solar-field electrical installation.

04

SMB Installation

Installation of SMB units within the solar-field electrical arrangement.

05

AC / DC Cable Laying

Laying AC and DC cables across the relevant sections of the solar installation.

06

SMB & Inverter Terminations

Termination work at SMB and inverter ends to complete the connected cable system.

07

MC4 & MCS Connector Crimping

Crimping MC4 and MCS connectors for the solar-field electrical connections.

08

Megger, Polarity & VOC Testing

Testing at both ends using megger, polarity and VOC checks as part of system verification.

09

MS Flat Laying

Laying MS flat sections including 25×3 and 25×6 sizes as required for the project.

10

Inverter Installation

Installation of inverters within the solar project electrical system.

11

MMS Installation

Installation of the module mounting structure system supporting the solar modules.

12

Transformer Erection

Erection of transformer equipment as part of the project electrical infrastructure.

13

HT Panel Erection

Erection of HT panels for the high-voltage electrical side of the project.

14

Transformer & HT Panel Testing

Testing of transformer and HT panel systems as part of project verification.
