Solar Ground Mounted Work System

Complete ground-mounted solar infrastructure covering site planning, generation systems, electrical rooms, high-voltage equipment and supporting structures.

Ground MountedSolar Infrastructure

Ground mounted systems & infrastructure

Each area below represents an important part of the ground-mounted work system.

01

Land Plan

Site planning establishes the arrangement of the solar field and supporting infrastructure across the available project land.

02

Solar Plant

The central generation area where photovoltaic modules are arranged as the primary solar power-producing system.

03

Small Grid 11KV

Electrical distribution infrastructure associated with the 11KV side of the project and its connection to the wider system.

04

LT Panel Room

A dedicated low-voltage electrical area for panel equipment, distribution and control of connected systems.

05

Solar Tower Plant

Supporting solar-plant infrastructure that forms part of the project's larger generation and electrical arrangement.

06

1250 KV Transformer

Transformer infrastructure used to transfer electrical energy between voltage levels within the project system.

07

110 KV Power Station

High-voltage power-station infrastructure supporting the project's electrical transmission and grid-side connection.

08

Solar Structure Work

Structural work that supports and positions the solar modules across the ground-mounted installation.

09

Switch Gear

Switching and protection equipment used to control, isolate and manage electrical circuits within the installation.
